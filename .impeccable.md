# Design Context

> 仅供参考：本文件提供界面设计背景，不替代现有主题、组件实现和 `docs/uiux-todo.md`。

Use this file with `PRODUCT.md` and `docs/uiux-todo.md` when shaping or auditing frontend work.

## Audience

Technical reviewers use the internal workspace and compare viewer to judge frame-level image differences quickly and reliably.

## Direction

Keep the interface precise, calm, dark-first, and work-focused. It should feel like an inspection workbench rather than a marketing page, generic SaaS dashboard, or media toy.

## Design Rules

- Keep the comparison image and selected target obvious at all times.
- Use clear boundaries, readable hierarchy, and complete interaction states.
- Preserve review capability across narrow and wide screens; do not hide core controls just to simplify layout.
- Keep first-use guidance brief and contextual.
- Make inline edits stable: no layout jump, full-page flash, or ambiguous save state.
- Use motion and decoration only when they clarify state or improve confidence.

## Avoid

- Decorative dashboards, oversized marketing language, and stock-like empty states.
- Loud neon, heavy glass effects, excessive animation, and playful UI language.
